# Problem Statement
Vehicle owners often struggle to maintain a centralized, accessible log of their vehicle's service history, modifications, and recurring mechanical issues. Generic notes apps lack structured data entry for specific automotive needs.

# Scope of the Project
MotoTrack is a Java-based Command Line Interface (CLI) application designed to manage garage inventory and track maintenance logs. It allows users to register vehicles, log specific service events or modifications, and generate service history reports. 

# Target Users
* Automotive enthusiasts and DIY mechanics.
* Vehicle owners wanting to track custom modifications.
* Small garage owners needing a lightweight tracking system.

# High-Level Features
* **Garage Management:** Add, view, and manage multiple vehicles (Make, Model, Year).
* **Maintenance Logging:** Record specific service events, parts replaced, and custom modifications.
* **Diagnostics Reporting:** Generate and view a chronological history of all work done on a specific vehicle.
